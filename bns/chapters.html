The twenty chapters

The Sanhita is divided into twenty chapters, running from the definitions in Chapter I to the repeal of the IPC in Chapter XX. Expand any chapter to see its range and what it covers.

Chapter numbers are Roman, section numbers are not

Chapters are numbered I to XX in the Act. Sections run in a single continuous sequence from 1 to 358 — a section's chapter is determined by its number, not printed alongside it in the text.
