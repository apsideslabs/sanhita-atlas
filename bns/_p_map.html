IPC to BNS: the correspondence

Forty provisions of the Indian Penal Code and where they landed in the Sanhita. The mapping is not one-to-one — several IPC sections were merged, split or reworded rather than carried across.

39 mappingsIPC 1860 → BNS 2023
IPCOffence BNSOffenceNote
120BCriminal conspiracy61(2)Criminal conspiracyPunishment now graded by the gravity of the offence conspired at.
124ASedition152Acts endangering sovereigntySedition is gone as a label; the substance is recast and extended to secession and armed rebellion.
141Unlawful assembly189Unlawful assemblySame five-person threshold; the clauses defining common object are redrawn.
147Rioting191RiotingDefinition carried forward; punishment now in sub-section (2).
153APromoting enmity between groups196Promoting enmityGrounds extended to include 'language' alongside religion, race and place of birth.
193Punishment for false evidence229False evidenceConsolidated with the fabricating provision.
201Causing disappearance of evidence238Causing disappearance of evidenceCarried forward with a higher ceiling where a capital offence is screened.
272Adulteration of food or drink274Adulteration of foodRetained, with a new clause covering adulteration intended for sale.
292Sale of obscene books294Obscene acts and songsObscenity provisions regrouped within Chapter XV.
295Injuring place of worship298Defiling place of worshipCarried forward.
295AOutraging religious feelings299Outraging religious feelingsCarried forward.
302Murder103(1)Punishment for murderDeath or life; the death penalty is no longer mandatory in any case.
304Culpable homicide not amounting to murder105Punishment for culpable homicideStructure retained across the two parts.
304ADeath by negligence106Causing death by negligenceBase punishment raised; a new sub-section (2) punishes a driver who flees the scene.
307Attempt to murder109Attempt to murderCarried forward, with hurt caused as the aggravating factor.
309Attempt to suicide226Attempt to commit suicideImprisonment replaced by a presumption of severe stress and a duty to rehabilitate.
323Voluntarily causing hurt115(2)Voluntarily causing hurtCarried forward; community service available as a sentence.
324Hurt by dangerous weapons118(1)Hurt by dangerous weaponsCarried forward.
325Voluntarily causing grievous hurt117(2)Voluntarily causing grievous hurtThe eight categories of grievous hurt are retained.
326Grievous hurt by dangerous weapons119(2)Grievous hurt by dangerous weaponsCarried forward.
326AAcid attack124Hurt by acid attackMinimum sentence of ten years retained.
354Assault on a woman to outrage modesty74Assault on a womanCarried forward.
354ASexual harassment75Sexual harassmentCarried forward with the same four clauses.
354DStalking78StalkingCarried forward, including monitoring internet use.
363Kidnapping137(2)Punishment for kidnappingDefinition and punishment consolidated into one section.
364AKidnapping for ransom140(2)Kidnapping for ransomDeath or life retained.
370Trafficking143Trafficking of a personGraded punishments by age and number of victims retained.
376Punishment for rape64Punishment for rapeMinimum of ten years to life retained.
379Theft303(2)Punishment for theftCommunity service added for a first conviction of small-value theft.
392Robbery309(1)RobberyCarried forward.
395Dacoity310(1)DacoityCarried forward.
406Criminal breach of trust316(2)Criminal breach of trustCarried forward.
411Receiving stolen property317(2)Receiving stolen propertyCarried forward.
420Cheating318(4)CheatingPunishment now graded by the value of the property and the harm caused.
465Forgery336(1)ForgeryCarried forward.
