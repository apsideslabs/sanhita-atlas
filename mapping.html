IPC to BNS

The provisions most often looked up, and where each one landed. Where a provision was substantively rewritten, the note says so rather than implying equivalence.

IPCOffenceBNSProvisionNote
302Murder103Punishment for murderDeath or life; the death penalty is no longer mandatory in any case.
304Culpable homicide not amounting to murder105Punishment for culpable homicideStructure retained across the two parts.
304ADeath by negligence106Causing death by negligenceBase punishment raised; a new sub-section (2) punishes a driver who flees the scene.
307Attempt to murder109Attempt to murderCarried forward, with hurt caused as the aggravating factor.
309Attempt to suicide226Attempt to commit suicideImprisonment replaced by a presumption of severe stress and a duty to rehabilitate.
323Voluntarily causing hurt115Voluntarily causing hurtCarried forward; community service available.
325Grievous hurt117Voluntarily causing grievous hurtThe eight categories of grievous hurt are retained.
326AAcid attack124Hurt by acid attackMinimum sentence of ten years retained.
354Assault on a woman to outrage modesty74Assault on a womanCarried forward.
354ASexual harassment75Sexual harassmentCarried forward with the same four clauses.
354DStalking78StalkingCarried forward, including monitoring internet use.
363Kidnapping137KidnappingDefinition and punishment consolidated into one section.
364AKidnapping for ransom140Kidnapping for ransomDeath or life retained.
370Trafficking143Trafficking of a personGraded punishments by age and number of victims retained.
375Rape — definition63RapeThe marital exception survives, now framed by the wife's age.
376Punishment for rape64Punishment for rapeMinimum of ten years to life retained.
376DGang rape70Gang rapeTwenty years to life.
379Theft303TheftCommunity service added for a first conviction of small-value theft.
392Robbery309RobberyCarried forward.
395Dacoity310DacoityCarried forward.
406Criminal breach of trust316Criminal breach of trustCarried forward.
411Receiving stolen property317Receiving stolen propertyCarried forward.
420Cheating318CheatingPunishment now graded by the value of the property and the harm caused.
465Forgery336ForgeryCarried forward.
498ACruelty by husband or relatives85Cruelty by husband or relativesCarried forward, with the same explanation of cruelty.
499/500Defamation356DefamationCommunity service introduced as an alternative to imprisonment.
506Criminal intimidation351Criminal intimidationCarried forward, with graded punishment.
509Word or gesture insulting modesty79Insult to the modesty of a womanCarried forward.
120BCriminal conspiracy61Criminal conspiracyPunishment now graded by the gravity of the offence conspired at.
124ASedition152Act endangering sovereigntySedition is gone as a label; the substance is recast and extended to secession and armed rebellion.
141Unlawful assembly189Unlawful assemblySame five-person threshold; the clauses defining common object are redrawn.
147Rioting191RiotingDefinition carried forward; punishment now in sub-section (2).
153APromoting enmity between groups196Promoting enmityGrounds extended to include language alongside religion, race and place of birth.
201Causing disappearance of evidence238Causing disappearance of evidenceCarried forward.
