The twenty chapters

The Sanhita runs in one continuous sequence of 358 sections. These are the chapters those sections fall into.

Chapter I · ss. 1–3

Preliminary

Short title, application, definitions and general explanations.

Chapter II · ss. 4–13

Punishments

The seven punishments, commutation, fines and solitary confinement.

Chapter III · ss. 14–44

General Exceptions

Mistake, accident, infancy, unsoundness of mind, consent, necessity — and the right of private defence.

Chapter IV · ss. 45–62

Abetment, Criminal Conspiracy and Attempt

Liability for instigating, conspiring and attempting, including abetment that never ripens into the offence.

Chapter V · ss. 63–99

Offences against Woman and Child

Sexual offences, criminal force, marriage offences, miscarriage, trafficking and offences against children.

Chapter VI · ss. 100–146

Offences affecting the Human Body

Culpable homicide and murder, hurt, restraint and confinement, kidnapping and forced labour.

Chapter VII · ss. 147–158

Offences against the State

Waging war, conspiracy against the State, and acts endangering sovereignty.

Chapter VIII · ss. 159–168

Offences relating to the Army, Navy and Air Force

Abetting mutiny and seducing service personnel from their allegiance.

Chapter IX · ss. 169–177

Offences relating to Elections

Bribery, undue influence, personation and interference with elections.

Chapter X · ss. 178–188

Offences relating to Coin, Currency and Government Stamps

Counterfeiting coins and notes, and offences against government stamps.

Chapter XI · ss. 189–197

Offences against Public Tranquillity

Unlawful assembly, rioting, affray and promoting enmity.

Chapter XII · ss. 198–205

Offences by or relating to Public Servants

Disobedience of law, false records and unlawful trade by officials.

Chapter XIII · ss. 206–226

Contempt of the Lawful Authority of Public Servants

Obstruction, false information, resistance to apprehension and attempt to suicide.

Chapter XIV · ss. 227–269

False Evidence and Offences against Public Justice

Perjury, fabricated evidence, harbouring, escape and bail-jumping.

Chapter XV · ss. 270–297

Offences affecting Public Health, Safety, Decency and Morals

Nuisance, adulteration, obscenity and negligence with dangerous substances.

Chapter XVI · ss. 298–302

Offences relating to Religion

Defiling places of worship, outraging religious feelings and disturbing assemblies.

Chapter XVII · ss. 303–334

Offences against Property

Theft, snatching, extortion, robbery, dacoity, criminal breach of trust, cheating, mischief and trespass.

Chapter XVIII · ss. 335–350

Offences relating to Documents and Property Marks

Forgery, false documents and electronic records, counterfeit seals and property marks.

Chapter XIX · ss. 351–357

Criminal Intimidation, Insult, Annoyance and Defamation

Threats, intentional insult, statements conducing to public mischief and defamation.

Chapter XX · ss. 358–358

Repeal and Savings

Repeals the Indian Penal Code, 1860, with a savings clause for pending proceedings.
