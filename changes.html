What changed on 1 July 2024

The Sanhita is not a rewrite of the IPC so much as a renumbering with deliberate changes layered on top. These are the ones that alter outcomes.

Section 111

Organised crime

Continuing unlawful activity by a crime syndicate — kidnapping, extortion, land grabbing, contract killing, financial fraud, trafficking, cyber-crime and more.

Read the section →

Section 112

Petty organised crime

The smaller-scale counterpart: theft, snatching, cheating or unauthorised betting committed by a syndicate.

Read the section →

Section 113

Terrorist act

A standalone offence of doing an act intended to threaten the unity, integrity, sovereignty or security of India.

Read the section →

Section 101

Group murder on identity grounds

Section 101(2) covers murder by a group of five or more on grounds of race, caste, community, sex, place of birth or language.

Read the section →

Section 304

Snatching

Sudden seizure of movable property from a person or their possession.

Read the section →

Section 69

Sexual intercourse by deceitful means

Obtaining intercourse by deceitful means or a false promise of marriage, where the act does not amount to rape.

Read the section →

Twenty offences were added, nineteen provisions were dropped, punishments were raised for thirty-three offences and fines for eighty-three.
