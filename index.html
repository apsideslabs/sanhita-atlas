The first eight chapters cover the machinery of the code — definitions, punishments, general exceptions and abetment. The substantive offences begin at Chapter V.

How to use this platform

Sanhita Atlas is an independent index, not a substitute for the Act. Section numbers were renumbered in 2023 and the correspondence is not one-to-one — always check a provision against the Gazette text before relying on it. Read the methodology.
