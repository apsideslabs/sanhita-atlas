Punishments

General Exceptions

Mistake, accident, infancy, unsoundness of mind, consent, necessity — and the right of private defence.

Chapter IV · ss. 45–62

Abetment, Criminal Conspiracy and Attempt

Liability for instigating, conspiring and attempting, including abetment that never ripens into the offence.

Offences affecting the Human Body

Culpable homicide and murder, hurt, restraint and confinement, kidnapping and forced labour.

How to read this

Every section page carries the text of the provision. Twenty-one of them — the ones that carry the most weight in practice — also carry an explanation, a worked example and the leading cases. Those cases were decided under the Indian Penal Code, which the Sanhita replaced; they remain persuasive on the same language.
